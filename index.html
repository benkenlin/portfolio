About Me

I am a Data Engineer and Machine Learning enthusiast with a strong academic and research background in Computer Science, Cloud Computing, and Quantitative Analytics. I hold an MSc in Computer Science & Engineering and am currently pursuing an MSc in Financial Engineering at WorldQuant University. My interests lie at the intersection of data, technology, and decision-making, where I enjoy transforming complex problems into scalable and practical solutions.

My professional journey began in technical support and laboratory administration before progressing into academia as a university lecturer. Over the years, I have taught a variety of computer science courses, mentored students, and contributed to research in distributed computing and workflow optimisation. My master's research focused on Data-Intensive Scientific Workflow Scheduling on Heterogeneous Cloud Computing Resources, exploring efficient resource allocation and performance optimisation in large-scale cloud environments.

Beyond academics, I am passionate about building data-driven applications and continuously expanding my expertise in Data Engineering, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, and Financial Engineering. I enjoy working with Python, SQL, cloud technologies, and modern data tools to develop solutions that create real-world impact. I am particularly interested in opportunities where data, research, and technology come together to drive innovation and solve meaningful challenges.

Education

My academic journey has been centred on building a strong foundation in computing, analytical thinking, and research. I earned a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from Ambo University, where I developed core knowledge in programming, database systems, software engineering, computer networks, and operating systems. During my undergraduate studies, I completed a capstone project titled "Android-Based Afaan Oromo Text-to-Speech Synthesizer", which focused on applying mobile computing and speech technology to support local language accessibility and digital inclusion.

I later pursued a Master of Science in Computer Science & Engineering from Adama Science and Technology University , where I expanded my expertise in advanced computing systems, cloud technologies, distributed computing, and research methodologies. My master's thesis, "Data-Intensive Scientific Workflow Scheduling on Heterogeneous Cloud Computing Resources", investigated efficient scheduling strategies for large-scale scientific workflows, with a focus on resource optimisation, performance improvement, and cloud computing environments. This research strengthened my interest in data-intensive systems, large-scale computing, and performance engineering.

Currently, I am pursuing a Master of Science in Financial Engineering at WorldQuant University. This interdisciplinary programme is enhancing my understanding of quantitative finance, statistical modelling, risk analysis, machine learning, and data-driven decision making. By combining my background in computer science with financial engineering, I am developing the analytical and technical skills required to solve complex problems across technology, data, and finance.

Experience

My professional experience spans higher education, research, technical support, and academic mentorship. Since June 2026, I have been serving as a Lecturer at Ethiopian Public Service University (EPSU), where I contribute to the development of future technology professionals through teaching, curriculum development, and student engagement. In this role, I deliver undergraduate computer science courses, develop instructional materials, and participate in academic programme enhancement initiatives that support modern computing education.

Prior to joining EPSU, I served as a Lecturer at Ambo University from September 2021 to June 2026. During this period, I taught a range of undergraduate courses including Database Systems, OOP, Artificial Intelligence, Computer Networks, Computer Organization and Architecture, Microprocessor and Assembly Language Programming, Compiler Design, Operating Systems, and Data Structures and Algorithm. Beyond classroom instruction, I supervised student projects, mentored capstone teams, conducted laboratory sessions, and contributed to curriculum improvement activities. This experience strengthened my abilities in communication, leadership, problem-solving, and the translation of complex technical concepts into practical learning experiences.

Before becoming a lecturer, I worked as a Technical Assistant in the Computer Laboratory at Ambo University from November 2016 to October 2021. In this role, I was responsible for administering laboratory infrastructure, maintaining Windows and Linux environments, configuring software and development tools, and providing technical support to students and faculty. I also assisted in practical computing sessions covering programming, databases, networking, and operating systems. This experience provided a strong foundation in system administration, troubleshooting, user support, and IT operations while fostering a deep appreciation for reliable and efficient computing environments.

Throughout my career, I have combined teaching, technical expertise, and research to support learning, innovation, and technology adoption. My experience working in both academic and technical environments has enabled me to develop a well-rounded perspective that bridges theory and practice, particularly in areas such as data engineering, cloud computing, machine learning, distributed systems, and technology-driven problem solving.
